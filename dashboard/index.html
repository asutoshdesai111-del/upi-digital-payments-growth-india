    The dataset behind every chart

    Every monthly record in your selected period. Sort by any column, search, or export what you see. This table is the accessible twin of every chart above.
    About this data. Monthly UPI volumes are rounded reference figures based on NPCI’s published UPI statistics and have been calibrated so each fiscal year adds up to NPCI’s reported annual totals. Monthly value comes from volume × an interpolated average ticket size, calibrated the same way. Banks-live and app-share figures are approximate. They were not downloaded live from NPCI, so please verify against NPCI’s official statistics before using them outside this project.
